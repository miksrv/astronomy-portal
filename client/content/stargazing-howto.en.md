Every stargazing trip is unique, but we follow a general format to make the event enjoyable and comfortable for all participants.

## Event announcement

Stargazing trips in Orenburg depend on good weather, so the exact date is only confirmed 2-3 days before the event. We announce upcoming trips in advance in our ["Look at the Stars"](https://t.me/look_at_stars) Telegram channel, where we post announcements and important updates. Subscribing to the channel is a must so you don't miss the next trip.

## Registering for a stargazing trip

Participation requires advance registration through our website - the form itself is always free and takes about a minute. Some trips are paid: the adult ticket is paid right on the site by card (kids under 18 always go free), and you'll get a QR ticket after payment. Official payment only happens through our website - we never ask you to transfer money to a card, in private messages, or through a third party. If anyone asks you to pay that way, it's a scam - don't send money, and let us know.

## Location

Stargazing trips take place out of town, away from city light pollution, usually about 40 km from Orenburg. The exact location is revealed after registration. You'll need to make your own way to the site, so check the route in advance. The final stretch is a dirt road about 300 meters long.

## Trip schedule

1. ### Arrival

    Arrive an hour to an hour and a half before the start, so you can calmly find a comfortable spot in the observing field and get settled. We usually begin the program right around sunset, so you'll have time to watch it too. Bring camping chairs or mats for comfort.

2. ### Astronomy talk

    As soon as dusk falls, our astronomy talk begins. We show videos and photos on a big screen, talking about space in an accessible and engaging way. Topics vary - from "The Perseid Meteor Shower" to "What Black Holes Hide". Talks are suitable for participants aged 8 and up and last about an hour.

3. ### Guided Tour of the Night Sky

    After the talk, we turn off the screen and switch off every light at the site - true darkness sets in, and the whole starry sky opens up overhead. Using laser pointers, our hosts point out constellations and the brightest stars, explaining what's where and how to find your way around the sky without any equipment - just by memory and a few key stars. This part lasts about 20 minutes.

4. ### Telescope observations

    Several telescopes are set up at the observing site, each pointed at a specific celestial object. You'll be able to look through the telescopes, ask questions and chat with the astronomers.

## Event duration

The whole trip takes 2 to 4 hours. You can leave at any time, but many stay until the very end, enjoying the view of the starry sky.

## Essential recommendations

Before the trip, be sure to read the ["Rules of Conduct on Stargazing Trips"](/stargazing/rules). On the field nobody turns on bright lights, makes a fire or makes noise: this way everyone's eyes adapt to the dark and everyone can look through a telescope in peace. A few simple rules make the evening comfortable and safe for all.

## Themed evenings

Besides the trips out of town, we hold themed evenings: meteor shower nights such as the Perseids, open-air film screenings and sidewalk astronomy right in the centre of Orenburg, when the telescopes stand on Belovka by the Chkalov monument and any passer-by can look at the Moon and the planets. You can read more about the city sessions in the ["Where to Look Through a Telescope in Orenburg"](/stargazing/where) section.
